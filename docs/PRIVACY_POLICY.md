# Navi Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-31

Version: privacy-2026-05-31

This draft is product-facing language for Navi. It must be reviewed by qualified privacy counsel before production use, especially if Navi is offered by or for a covered entity, healthcare provider, researcher, university, employer, insurer, or other regulated organization.

## Sensitive Data Navi Handles

Navi may store journal text, mood ratings, sentiment analysis, audio recordings, audio metadata, transcripts, WHOOP-derived wellness metrics, optional in-app typing rhythm aggregates, predictions, insight outputs, account identifiers, device sync status, and consent preferences. Mental health entries can be highly sensitive even when they are not part of a clinical record.

## How Data Is Used

Navi uses data to provide journaling, mood tracking, audio analysis, personalized insights, longitudinal trends, model predictions, account sync, support, security monitoring, data export, and deletion workflows.

Navi should not use identifiable health or wellbeing data for advertising, unrelated profiling, sale, or third-party marketing.

## Cloud Sync and Processors

When cloud sync is enabled, app data may be stored in Firebase services and processed by the Navi backend. Cloud storage, authentication, logging, analytics, model hosting, monitoring, and support vendors must be reviewed for security, privacy, data location, retention, and business associate agreement requirements before production use with regulated health data.

## Optional Research Sharing

Research/data-sharing is optional and separate from app use. When enabled, Navi may send de-identified or coded feature records for model improvement and possible academic research. Optional typing rhythm tracking uses aggregate metadata such as typing cadence, pauses, correction rate, and platform category; Navi should not store raw typed content, individual typed characters, passwords, or full key event timelines for this feature.

Do not treat the in-app research toggle as IRB-approved human-subjects research consent until an IRB or qualified ethics reviewer has approved the protocol, consent language, data plan, withdrawal process, and recruitment process.

## User Controls

Users can change cloud sync, personalized insights, and research/data-sharing settings. Users can export or delete their data from the app. Deletion removes local app data and requests deletion from connected cloud/backend stores, although backups, audit logs, legal holds, and already de-identified aggregate datasets may have separate retention rules.

## Safety and Crisis Limits

Navi is not emergency care, a crisis monitoring service, a therapist, a physician, or a substitute for professional diagnosis or treatment. If a user may harm themselves or someone else, they should call emergency services or a crisis line immediately.

## HIPAA Status

HIPAA may apply depending on how Navi is offered, who operates it, whether covered entities are involved, and whether Navi acts as a business associate. This policy is written for HIPAA-oriented handling but is not a legal determination that Navi is HIPAA compliant.
